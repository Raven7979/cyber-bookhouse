# Demo asset notes

- `quick-demo.gif` is an eight-second excerpt of the full screen demo. It uses
  public webpages and a dedicated, sanitized Obsidian demo vault.
- `cyber-sanwei-demo.mp4` contains the VoiceBox narration only. Background
  music and sound effects were removed because their public-release provenance
  was not documented.
- `real-obsidian-result.png` is a screenshot of an actual Obsidian window, not
  a recreated product interface. The vault contains demonstration data only.
- `before-after.svg` is the editable source for `before-after.png`.

Third-party product names and page screenshots appear only to explain compatible
content sources and workflows. Their marks remain the property of their owners.
